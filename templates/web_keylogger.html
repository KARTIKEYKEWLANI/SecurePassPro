Protecting Against Keyloggers

Use Updated Software

Keep your operating system and browsers updated to protect against known vulnerabilities that keyloggers exploit.

Virtual Keyboards

For sensitive inputs, use virtual keyboards that prevent hardware keyloggers from capturing your keystrokes.

Two-Factor Authentication

Even if a keylogger captures your password, 2FA provides an additional layer of security that's much harder to bypass.

Be Cautious

Avoid downloading software from untrusted sources and be wary of phishing attempts that may install keyloggers.
